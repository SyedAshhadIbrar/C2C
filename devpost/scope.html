Chaos to Clarity

A web app that turns messy meeting notes into a reliable, editable action plan, without making up anything the notes don't say.

The unique kernel

Reliable over polished. Confirmed tasks are kept separate from unclear information. Missing deadlines, owners, or decisions show as Not specified and are flagged for clarification instead of guessed. Every task shows the original source phrase from the notes that supports it.

Who it's for

A developer or project lead on a small software team, just out of a project-planning meeting. The notes are informal and scattered: features, bug fixes, responsibilities, dates, vague commitments, and unresolved dependencies. Today they'd reread the whole conversation.

Feel

Satisfying, like turning a cluttered desk into an organized workspace. Notes transform into task cards, blockers are highlighted, and open questions are separated from confirmed decisions.

Why it matters

"People often leave meetings with lots of information but no clear plan."

Goal: a portfolio-quality demo, and learning how AI-generated structured data works and how to validate it reliably.
